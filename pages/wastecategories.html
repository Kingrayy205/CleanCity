Waste Categories

Learn about different types of waste and how to dispose of them properly.

Plastics include bottles, containers, and packaging. Always check local recycling guidelines. ♻️ Tip: Rinse containers before recycling.

Organic waste includes food scraps and yard trimmings. 🌱 Tip: Compost organic waste to create natural fertilizer.

E-waste includes electronics like phones, computers, and batteries. ⚡ Tip: Drop off e-waste at certified recycling centers.

Paper waste includes newspapers, books, and office papers. 📄 Tip: Keep paper dry and separate from greasy items before recycling.

Glass includes bottles and jars. Avoid mixing with ceramics or broken glass. 🍾 Tip: Reuse jars at home before recycling.
