What You Should know About Recycling

🌊
Plastic pollution harms oceans:

About 8 million tons of plastic end up in oceans each year, threatening marine life.

🧑‍🏭
Recycling supports jobs:

In the U.S., the recycling industry supports 750,000 jobs and generates billions in annual revenue.

🌳
Paper recycling saves forests:

Recycling 1 ton of paper saves about 17 trees and 7,000 gallons of water.

♻️
Recycling saves energy:

Producing new aluminum from recycled cans uses 95% less energy than making it from raw bauxite ore.

🍂
Organic waste creates methan:

Food waste in landfills produces methane, a greenhouse gas 25 times more potent than CO₂.
